My Story

I know schools from the classroom to the central office.

My jobs changed. The basic work did not: help people do their best work for students.

I began as an English teacher, spent five years as a high school assistant principal, and served eight years as principal of Henley Middle School. When Western Albemarle High School had an unexpected vacancy, I stepped in as principal for a year while continuing my division leadership role. Since 2015, I have worked in central office, first in strategic planning, then as Chief of Strategic Planning, and now as Assistant Superintendent.

“People should know what matters, why it matters, what they are responsible for, and how we will know if the work is helping students.”

Selected Leadership Work

What I have actually led.

School Improvement

Focused support for schools with the greatest needs

I developed a more disciplined improvement approach built around a small number of measurable priorities, biweekly principal coaching, 90-day plans, targeted staffing and expertise, and frequent evidence reviews.

  • Principal supervision in high-need schools
  • Transformation Schools support model
  • Short-cycle planning and progress checks

Teaching & Learning

Turning an outside audit into changes teachers could use

After an independent instructional audit, I helped lead implementation of clearer K–12 pacing, common quarterly assessments, stronger use of student data, aligned professional learning, and program evaluation.

Strategy & Community

Building a strategic plan with the community, not for it

I directed development of Learning for All through a 112-member design team, public forums, surveys, School Board work, and measurable implementation goals.

People

Selecting, coaching, evaluating, and supporting leaders

I serve on the cabinet-level principal hiring team, evaluate principals in schools with significant academic challenges, manage performance matters, and lead a department with one of the division’s strongest Gallup engagement results.

Evaluation

Asking whether programs are actually worth continuing

I created a dedicated Program Evaluation function and founded a twice-yearly Budget Initiative Review Team to examine implementation, results, cost, and whether investments should continue, change, expand, or end.

Board & Public Decisions

Listening first, explaining the choice, and closing the loop

I have planned Board retreats, facilitated work sessions, led Parent Council, supervised Strategic Communications, and led a redistricting process in which public feedback changed the preferred option.

Resources & Operations

Connecting priorities to money, people, buildings, and future costs

My work includes budget prioritization, health-care forecasting, capital-project review, enrollment projections, staffing decisions, and school safety planning.

How I Lead

Simple expectations. Useful support. Honest accountability.

01

Listen before deciding.

02

Keep the number of priorities small.

03

04

Use evidence to see what is working and change course when needed.

05

Explain decisions plainly and follow through.
